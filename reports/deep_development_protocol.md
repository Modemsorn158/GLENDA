# Extended development-only training

This exploratory experiment was planned on 21 November 2026, after the original internal holdout had already been consumed and its results disclosed. It does not evaluate that holdout or supply an independent estimate of generalization. Unknown patient links, correlated frames, source imbalance and differences in fold difficulty remain important limitations. The v2 development folds were used in previous exploratory work; these results must not be presented as a fresh or nested assessment.

The comparison in [deep_development.json](../configs/deep_development.json) runs ImageNet-pretrained EfficientNet-B0 with frozen, partial and full feature fine-tuning on the same five development folds. All three use weighted loss, learning rate 0.0001, batch size 16, at most 30 epochs and early stopping after seven consecutive epochs without improvement in validation average precision. The fixed learning rate helps isolate fine-tuning depth relative to the earlier tuning comparison, but it is not necessarily optimal for each depth. All other defaults, including the square resize and horizontal flip, are unchanged. Results remain subject to adaptive early stopping and model selection, and do not establish convergence or clinical usefulness.

The completed search is recorded in `outputs/search/deep_development`, including source fingerprints, the audited inventory digest, fold configurations, training histories, validation scores and checkpoints. Partial-fine-tuning folds and full-fine-tuning folds 1–4 ran concurrently with frozen folds using the same declared configurations; completion statuses were recorded for the main search to resume. Full-fine-tuning fold zero was started separately with the same settings and incorporated as that fold after checking its configuration, source hashes, epoch history, predictions and checkpoint; it is not an additional fold. Image-level artifacts and predictions remain local and ignored by Git. The existing holdout, original model and ledger are unchanged.

Report completed and failed folds, epochs and validation average precision, as well as any hardware or runtime interruptions. Development AP is a selection statistic, not external validation. No new decision threshold, calibration method or test evaluation is implied by this experiment.

All 15 planned folds completed without a recorded failure, for 195 total training epochs. The [results](deep_development_results.md) include the best epoch, total epochs and AP for every fold, plus descriptive score diagnostics. The summary was regenerated from the recorded search, development manifest, epoch histories, predictions and checkpoints using `python -m glenda.summarize_development outputs\search\deep_development outputs\splits\v2\manifest.csv reports\deep_development_results.md`; it verifies development-fold coverage and recomputes AP before writing aggregate results. This requires the private local artifacts; no test images or predictions are read.
