# Protocol for a future independent study

The completed GLENDA holdout is consumed. Its low sensitivity, poor score calibration, three negative-bearing groups, source imbalance and unknown patient links remain limitations of the published model. Do not use its results or images to choose another threshold, model, calibrator, preprocessing setting, training duration or cohort partition.

Before a new study, obtain authoritative patient-to-video mappings and sufficient independent positive and negative cases. Document missing links and unresolved duplicate candidates. Assemble a genuinely external cohort, with institutional and acquisition details, under appropriate permissions. The current release cannot establish either patient independence or external validity by itself. Report negative-bearing patients or sources, their frame counts, and valid bootstrap replicates; a degenerate empirical interval is not evidence of perfect specificity.

Write and date a protocol before examining the new evaluation data. Specify the research application, decision unit, intended population, class definitions, minimum acceptable sensitivity and error-cost tradeoffs with the relevant domain experts. No numeric clinical requirement has been established for this repository. Freeze the model, preprocessing, calibration, decision threshold and analysis plan before external evaluation. Preserve cohort-level independence and report frame-, video-, and patient-level results where labels and mappings permit. Use independent patient-level or source-level uncertainty estimates when enough independent units exist; report when data are insufficient.

Use grouped development data for model comparisons, with controlled one-factor or factorial experiments for fine-tuning depth, learning rate, aspect-ratio-preserving padding versus the historical stretch, and appropriate augmentation. Prespecify a training budget and inspect learning curves and convergence; the historical two-candidate, three-epoch search does not isolate these effects. If an unbiased estimate of the adaptive development procedure is needed, perform nested grouped cross-validation with model selection, stopping, calibration, and threshold selection contained within each outer training fold. All splits must respect verified patients and duplicate links.

Reserve a separate, patient-disjoint calibration set or use a rigorously nested cross-fitting design. Assess calibration and the precision–recall tradeoff without using the consumed holdout; validate the final calibrator and operating policy on independent cases. `select_threshold --min-sensitivity VALUE` provides an optional development-only rule: it selects the highest observed threshold meeting the specified pooled sensitivity. The requirement must be specified before examining relevant selection outcomes, and its apparent sensitivity is not an independent performance estimate. The default remains historical F1 selection, not a clinical recommendation. Pooling scores from different models can still lead to threshold-transfer failure; compare calibration and score distributions of refits on independent development data.

For leakage and shortcut analysis, supplement dHash with validated crop/resize-robust similarity methods, review candidate pairs manually without using final test outcomes, and document both false matches and misses. Compare source, device, lighting, frame selection and acquisition distributions within classes where metadata allow; evaluate on source-controlled cohorts and report unresolved confounding. More independent cases and mappings must be obtained before these analyses can establish generalization.

Use an independently preserved audit inventory and its trusted SHA-256 for the command-line integrity checks. Freeze candidate configuration, source fingerprints, validation predictions, epoch histories and the chosen threshold before final fitting. Training and evaluation commands fail when the trusted inventory does not match the recorded inputs. These controls cannot prevent data changing after verification and do not establish that patient identifiers are complete. The earlier final fit and its old selection artifacts predate these controls and are preserved as historical evidence.
